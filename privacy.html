Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how The Unlit Room ("we," "us") handles information in connection with our website (the "Site") and Unlit Room Publisher, our internal first-party application used to publish our own content to our own accounts on third-party platforms such as TikTok.

1. Information We Collect on the Site

The Site is a static informational page. We do not require accounts, do not run our own analytics or advertising trackers, and do not set cookies. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send and use it only to respond.

2. Unlit Room Publisher and Platform Accounts

Unlit Room Publisher is used exclusively by our own team to publish The Unlit Room's original episodes to The Unlit Room's own platform accounts. In connection with this:

  • When our team connects the show's TikTok account via TikTok's OAuth flow (Login Kit), we receive an access token and basic profile information for that account only (scope: user.info.basic).
  • We use TikTok's Content Posting API (scopes: video.upload, video.publish) solely to upload and publish our own original videos to that connected account.
  • Access tokens are stored securely on our own systems, are used only to operate the show's publishing pipeline, and are never sold or shared with third parties.

The application has no third-party users. We do not collect, store, or process personal information about anyone other than our own team's platform accounts.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information. Content we publish is distributed through third-party platforms (YouTube, TikTok, Spotify, Apple Podcasts, Buzzsprout), whose own privacy policies govern data they collect from their users.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Platform access tokens are retained only as long as needed to operate the publishing pipeline and can be revoked at any time from the connected platform account's settings. To request deletion of any data relating to you, contact us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days.

5. Children's Privacy

The Site and the application are not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time; the "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

7. Contact
